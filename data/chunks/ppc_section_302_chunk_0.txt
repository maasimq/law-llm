ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 302
TITLE: Punishment of qatl-i-amd:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Punishment of qatl-i-amd:
Whoever commits qatl-e-amd shall, subject to the
provisions of this Chapter be:
(a)
punished with death as qisas;
(b)
punished with death or imprisonment for life as ta'zir having regard to the facts and
circumstances of the case, if the proof in either of the forms specified in Section 304 is not
available; or
(c)
punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to
twenty-five years, where according to the injunctions of Islam the punishment of qisas is
not applicable
