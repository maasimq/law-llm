514B. Bond required from a minor. When the person required by any Court or officer to
execute a bond is a minor, such Court or officer may accept, in lieu thereof, a bond executed by a
surety or sureties only.
 6[515. Appeals from, and revision of, orders under section 514. ­All orders passed by any
Magistrate under section 514 shall be appealable to the Sessions Judge or, if no appeal is preferred
against any such order, may be revised by the Sessions Judge".]
