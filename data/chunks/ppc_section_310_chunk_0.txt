(2)
Where a wali is a minor or an insane, the wali of such minor or insane wali may
compound the right of qisas on behalf of such minor or insane wali:
Provided
that the value of badf-i-sufh shall not be less than the value of diyat.
(3)
Where the Government is the wali, it may compound the right of qisas:
Provided
that fee value of badi-i-sulh shall not be less than the value of diyat.
(4)
Where the badl-i-sulh is not determined or is a property or a right the value of which
cannot be determined in terms of money under Shari'ah, the right of qisas shall be
deemed to have been compounded and the offender shall be liable to diyat.
(5)
Badl-i-sulh may be paid or given on demand or on a deferred date as may be agreed
upon between the offender and the wali.
Explanation:
In this section, Badl-i-sulh means the mutually agreed compensation
according to Shari'ah to be paid or given by the offender to a wali in cash or in kind or in
the form of movable or immovable property.
