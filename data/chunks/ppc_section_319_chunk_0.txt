ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 319
TITLE: Punishment for qatl-i-khata:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Punishment for qatl-i-khata:
Whoever commits qatl-i-khata shall be liable to diyat:
Provided
that, where qatl-i-khata is committed by a rash or negligent act, other than rash
or negligent driving, the offender may, in addition to diyat, also be punished with
imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to five years as ta'zir.
