CHAPTER: 
============================================================

440. Optional with Court to hear parties. No party has any right to be heard either personally or by
pleader before any Court when exercising its powers of revision :

 Provided that the Court may, if it thinks fit, when exercising such powers, hear any party either
personally or by pleader, and that nothing in this section shall be deemed to affect section 439, sub­section
(2).

ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 442
TITLE: High Court's order to be certified to lower Court or Magistrate. When a case is revised under
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

442. High Court's order to be certified to lower Court or Magistrate. When a case is revised under
this Chapter by the High Court, it shall, in manner hereinbefore provided by section 425, certify its decision
or order to the Court by which the finding, sentence or order revised was recorded or passed, and the Court
or Magistrate to which the decision or order is so certified shall thereupon make such orders as are
conformable to the decision so certified ; and, if necessary, the record shall be amended in
accordance therewith.
