ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 337G
TITLE: Hurt by rash or negligent driving:
CHAPTER: CHAPTER XVI - OF OFFENCES AFFECTING THE HUMAN BODY
============================================================

Hurt by rash or negligent driving:
Whoever causes hurt by rash or negligent driving or riding on any public way shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to five years, or with arsh or daman, or with both.
