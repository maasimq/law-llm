ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 383
TITLE: Execution of sentences of 1[imprisonment for life] or imprisonment in other
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

383. Execution of sentences of 1[imprisonment for life] or imprisonment in other
cases. Where the accused is sentenced to 1[imprisonment for life] or imprisonment in cases
other than those provided for by section 381, 2[and section 382­A], the Court passing the
sentence shall forthwith forward a warrant to the jail in which he is, or is to be, confined, and,
unless the accused is already confined in such jail, shall forward him to such jail, with the
warrant.


ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 384
TITLE: Direction of warrant for execution. Every warrant for the execution of a sentence
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

384. Direction of warrant for execution. Every warrant for the execution of a sentence
of imprisonment shall be directed to the officer in charge of the jail or other place in which
the prisoner is, or is to be, confined.
