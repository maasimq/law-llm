ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 305
TITLE: Wali:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Wali:
In case of qatl, the wali shall be--
(a)
the heirs of the victim, according to his personal law
136
[but shall not include the accused or the convict in case of qatl-i-amd if committed in the name or on the pretext of honour]
136
; and
(b)
the Government, if there is no heir.
