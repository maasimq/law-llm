ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 294
TITLE: Obscene acts and songs:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Obscene acts and songs:
Whoever, to the annoyance of others, --
(a)
does any obscene act in any public place, or
(b)
sings, recites or utters any obscene songs, ballad or words, in or near any public
place,
shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to
three months, or with fine, or with both.
