ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 318
TITLE: Qatl-i-khata:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Qatl-i-khata:
Whoever, without any intention to cause death of, or cause harm to, a
person causes death of such person, either by mistake of act or by mistake of fact, is said
to commit qatl-i-khata.
Illustrations
(a)
A aims at a deer but misses the target and kills Z who is standing by, A is guilty of
qatl-i-khata.
(b)
A shoots at an object to be a boar but it turns out to be a human being. A is guilty of
qatl-i-khata.
