ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 304
TITLE: Proof of qatl-i-amd liable to qisas, etc.:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Proof of qatl-i-amd liable to qisas, etc.:
(1)
Proof of qatl-i-amd shall be in any of the following forms, namely: -
(a)
the accused makes before a Court competent to try the offence a voluntary and true
confession of the commission of the offence; or
(b)
by the evidence as provided in Article 17 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat, 1984 (P.O. No. 10
of 1984).
(2)
The provisions of sub-section (1) shall, mutatis, mutandis, apply to a hurt liable to
qisas.
