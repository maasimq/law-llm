ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 79
TITLE: Warrant directed to police­officer. A warrant directed to any police­officer may also be executed
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

79. Warrant directed to police­officer. A warrant directed to any police­officer may also be executed
by any other police­officer whose name is endorsed upon the warrant by the officer to whom it is directed or
endorsed.
