9. Court of Session.­(1) The 7[Provincial Government] shall establish a Court of Session for every
sessions division, and appoint a Judge of such Court.

(2) The 7[Provincial Government] may, by general or special order in the official Gazette, direct at what
place or places the Court of Session shall hold its sitting; but, until such order is made, the Courts of Session
shall hold their sittings as heretofore.

(3) The 7[Provincial Government] may also appoint Additional Sessions Judges and Assistant Sessions
Judges to exercise jurisdiction in one or more such Courts.
