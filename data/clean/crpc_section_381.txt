ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 381
TITLE: Execution of order passed under section 376.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

381. Execution of order passed under section 376. When a sentence of death passed by a
Court of Session is submitted to the High Court for confirmation, such Court of Session shall, on
receiving the order of confirmation or other order of the High Court thereon, cause such order to be
carried into effect by issuing a warrant or taking such other steps as may be necessary 1[:]
1[Provided that the sentence of death shall not be executed if the heirs of the deceased pardon
the convict or enter into a compromise with him even at the last moment before execution of the
sentence].
