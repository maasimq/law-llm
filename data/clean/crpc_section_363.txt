ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 363
TITLE: Remarks respecting demeanour of witness.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

363. Remarks respecting demeanour of witness. When a Sessions Judge or Magistrate has
recorded the evidence of a witness, he shall also record such remarks (if any) as he thinks
material respecting the demeanour of such witness whilst under examination.
