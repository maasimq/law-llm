ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 312
TITLE: Qatl-i-amd after waiver or compounding of qisas:
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Qatl-i-amd after waiver or compounding of qisas:
Where a wali commits qatl-iamd
of a convict against whom the right of qisas has been waived under Section 309 or
compounded under Section 310, such wali shall be punished with-
(a)
qisas, if he had himself, waived or compounded the right of qisas against the convict or
had knowledge of such waiver of-composition by another wali, or
(b)
diyat, if he had no knowledge of such waiver or composition.
