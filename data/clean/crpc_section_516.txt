TITLE: Power to direct levy of amount due on certain recognizances.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

516. Power to direct levy of amount due on certain recognizances. The High Court or
Court of Session may direct any Magistrate to levy the amount due on a bond to appear and attend
at such High Court or Court of Session.
