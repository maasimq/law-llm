179. Accused triable in, district where act is done or where consequence ensues. When a Person is
accused of the Commission of any offence by reason of anything which has been done, and of any
consequence which has ensued, such offence may be inquired into or tried by a Court within the local limits
of whose jurisdiction any such thing has been done, or any such consequence has ensued.

 Illustrations

 (a) A is wounded within the local limits of the jurisdiction of Court X, and dies within the local limits
 of the jurisdiction of Court Z. The offence of the culpable homicide of A may be inquired into or
 tried by X or Z.

 (b) A is wounded within the local limits of the jurisdiction of Court X, and is, during ten days within
 the local limits of the jurisdiction of Court Y, and during ten days more within the local limits of
 the jurisdiction of Court Z, unable in the local limits of the jurisdiction of either Court Y, or
