ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 243
TITLE: Conviction on admission of truth of accusation.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

243. Conviction on admission of truth of accusation. If the accused admits that he has committed the
offence 2[with which he is charged], his admission shall be recorded as nearly as possible in the words used
by him; and, if he shows no sufficient cause why he should not be convicted, the Magistrate 3[may convict]
him accordingly.
