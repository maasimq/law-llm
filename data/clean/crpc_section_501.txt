501. Power to order sufficient bail when that first taken is insufficient. lf, through mistake, fraud or
otherwise, insufficient sureties have been accepted, or if they afterwards become insufficient, the Court may
issue a warrant of arrest directing that the person released on bail be brought before it and may order him to
find sufficient sureties, and, on his failing so to do, may commit him to Jail.
