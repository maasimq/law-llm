369. Court not to alter judgment. 7[Save as otherwise provided by this Code or by any other law for
the time being in force or, in the case of a High Court 8* * *, by the Letters Patent of such High Court, no
Court], when it has signed its judgment, shall alter or review the same, except 9* * *to correct a clerical
error.
