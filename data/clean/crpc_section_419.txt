ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 419
TITLE: Petition of appeal.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

419. Petition of appeal. Every appeal shall be made in the form of petition in writing presented by the
appellant or his pleader, and every such petition shall (unless the Court to which it is presented otherwise
directs) be accompanied by a copy of the judgment or order appealed against 2* * *.
