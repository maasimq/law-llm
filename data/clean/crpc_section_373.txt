TITLE: Court of Session to send copy of finding and sentence to 5[officer­in­charge of prosecution]. In
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

373. Court of Session to send copy of finding and sentence to 5[officer­in­charge of prosecution]. In
cases tried by the Court of Session, the Court shall forward a copy of its finding and sentence (if any) to the
5[officer­in­charge of prosecution in the district] within the local limits of whose Jurisdiction the trial was
held.
