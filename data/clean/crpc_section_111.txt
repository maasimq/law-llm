111. [Proviso as to European vagrants.] Rep. by the Criminal Law Amendment Act, 1923
(XII of'1923), s. 8.
 1,2112.
 Order to be made. When a Magistrate acting under section 107, section
108, section 109 or section 110 deems it necessary to require any person to show cause under
such section, he shall make an order in writing, setting forth the substance of the information
received, the amount of the bond to be executed, the term for which it is to be in force, and
the number, character and class of sureties (if any) required.
 1, 2113. Procedure in respect of person present in Court. If the person in respect of
whom such order is made is present in Court, it shall be read over to him or, if he so desires,
the substance thereof shall be explained to him.
