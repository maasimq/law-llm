ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 400
TITLE: Return of warrant on execution of sentence.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

400. Return of warrant on execution of sentence. When a sentence has been fully executed, the
officer executing it shall return the warrant to the Court from which it issued, with an endorsement under his
hand certifying the manner in which the sentence has been executed.
