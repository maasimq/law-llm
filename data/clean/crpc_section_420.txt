ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 420
TITLE: Procedure when appellant in jail. If the appellant is in jail, he may present his petition of appeal
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

420. Procedure when appellant in jail. If the appellant is in jail, he may present his petition of appeal
and the copies accompanying the same to the officer in charge of the jail, who shall thereupon forward such
petition and copies to the proper Appellate Court.
