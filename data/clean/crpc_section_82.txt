82. Where warrant may be executed. A warrant of arrest may be executed at any place in 3[Pakistan].
4[Explanation.­ In this section, “warrant of arrest” includes a warrant of arrest issued under this Code
as in force in Azad Jammu and Kashmir.].

1 The words and commas “and, when issued by a Presidency Magistrate, shall always be so directed” omitted by A.O., 1949, Sch.
2 Subs ibid., for “any other Court”.
3 Subs. by the Central Laws (Statue Reform) Ordinance, 1960 (21 of 1960), s.3 and 2nd Sch. (with effect from the 14th October, 1955), for “the Provinces and the Capital of the Federation”, which had
been subs. by A.O., 1949, Arts. 3 (2) and 4, for “British India”.
4 Added by Act. VIII of 1993, s.3, (w.e.f. 20­11­93, SRO­1106(1)/93, dated 16­11­93).
5 Subs. by Ord. 37 of 2001, s.23 (w.e.f. 14­8­2001).
