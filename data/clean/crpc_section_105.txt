ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 105
TITLE: Magistrate may direct search in his presence.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

105. Magistrate may direct search in his presence. Any Magistrate may direct a search to be made in
his presence of any place for the search of which he is competent to issue a search­warrant.
