ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 413
TITLE: No appeal in petty cases.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

413. No appeal in petty cases. Notwithstanding anything hereinbefore contained, there shall be no
appeal by a convicted person in cases in which 5[a High Court passes a sentence of imprisonment not
exceeding six months only or of fine not exceeding two hundred rupees only or in which] a Court of Session
6
* * *passes a sentence of imprisonment not exceeding one month only, or 7[in which a Court of Session or
8[a] Magistrate of the first class passes a sentence] of fine not exceeding fifty rupees only 9* * *.

Explanation.­ There is no appeal from a sentence of imprisonment passed by such Court or Magistrate
in default of payment of fine when no substantive sentence of imprisonment has also been passed.
