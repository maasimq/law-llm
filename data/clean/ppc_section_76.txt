ACT: Pakistan Penal Code, 1860
SECTION/ARTICLE: 76
TITLE: Section 76
CHAPTER: INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Act done by a person bound, or by mistake of fact believing himself bound, by
law:
Nothing is an offence which Is done by a person who is, or who by reason of a
mistake of fact and not reason of a mistake of law in good faith believes himself to be,
bound by law to do it.
Illustrations
(a)
A, a soldier, fires on a mob by the order of his superior officer, in conformity, with the
commands of the law. A has committed no offence.
(b)
A an officer of a Court of Justice, being ordered by that Court to arrest Y and after due
enquiry, believing Z to be Y arrests Z. A has committed no offence.
