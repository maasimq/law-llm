ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 3125
TITLE: Power of District Magistrate to cancel any bond for keeping the peace or good.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

3125. Power of District Magistrate to cancel any bond for keeping the peace or good

behaviour. The 4* * * 1[Sessions Judge] may at any time, for sufficient reasons to be recorded in
writing, cancel any bond for keeping the peace or for good behaviour executed under this Chapter
by order of any Court in his district not superior to his Court.
