353. Evidence to be taken in presence of accused. Except as otherwise expressly provided, all evidence taken
under 4[Chapters XX, XXI, XXII and XXIIA] shall be taken in the presence of the accused, or, when his personal
attendance is dispensed with, in presence of his pleader.
