ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 343
TITLE: No influence to be used to induce disclosures.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

343. No influence to be used to induce disclosures. Except as provided in sections 337 and
338, no influence, by means of any promise or threat or otherwise, shall be used to an accused
person to induce him to disclose or withhold any matter within his knowledge.
