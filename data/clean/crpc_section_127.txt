127. Assembly to disperse on command of Magistrate or police­officer.__(1) Any 2* * * officer in
charge of a police station may command any unlawful assembly, or any assembly of five or more persons
likely to cause a disturbance of the public peace, to disperse ; and it shall thereupon be the duty of the
members of such assembly to disperse accordingly.
