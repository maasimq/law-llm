379. Procedure in cases submitted to High Court for confirmation. In cases submitted by the Court
of Session to the High Court for the confirmation of a sentence of death, the proper officer of the High
Court shall, without delay, after the order of confirmation or other order has been made by the High Court,
send a copy of the order, under the seal of the High Court and attested with his official signature, to the
Court of Session.
