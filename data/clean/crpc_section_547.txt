ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 547
TITLE: Moneys ordered to be paid recoverable as fines. Any money (other than a fine) payable by
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

547. Moneys ordered to be paid recoverable as fines. Any money (other than a fine) payable by
virtue of any order made under this Code, 3[and the method of recovery of which is not otherwise expressly
provided for] shall be recoverable as if it were a fine.
