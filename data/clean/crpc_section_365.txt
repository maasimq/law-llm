TITLE: Record of evidence in High Court.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

365. Record of evidence in High Court. Every High Court 2* * * 3* * * 4* * * 5[shall] from time to
time, by general rule prescribe the manner in which evidence shall be taken down in cases coming before
the Court, 6[and the evidence shall be taken down in accordance with such rule].
