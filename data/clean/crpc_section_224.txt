ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 224
TITLE: Words in charge taken in sense of law under which offence is punishable. In every
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

224. Words in charge taken in sense of law under which offence is punishable. In every
charge words used in describing an offence shall be deemed to have been used in the sense
attached to them respectively by the law under which such offence is punishable.
