156B. Investigation against a woman accused of the offence of zina.___Notwithstanding anything contained in
this Code, where a person is accused of offence of zina under the Offence of zina (Enforcement of Hudood)
Ordinance, 1979 (VII of 1979), no police officer below the rank of a Superintendent of Police shall investigate such
offence nor shall such accused be arrested without permission of the court.

 Explanation.­ In this section 'zina' does not include ‘zina­bil­jabr’.]
