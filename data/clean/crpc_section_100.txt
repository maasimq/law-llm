ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 100
TITLE: Search for persons wrongfully confined.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

100. Search for persons wrongfully confined. If any 6* *, Magistrate of the first class 8[* * *] has
reason to believe that any person is confined under such circumstances that the confinement amounts to an
offence, he may issue a search­warrant, and the person to whom such warrant is directed may search for the
person so confined; and such search shall be made in accordance therewith, and the person, if found, shall
be immediately taken before a Magistrate, who shall make such order as in the circumstances of the case
seems proper.
