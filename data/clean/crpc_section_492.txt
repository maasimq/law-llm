TITLE: Power to appoint Public Prosecutors.­(1) The 5* * 6[Provincial Government] may appoint,
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

492. Power to appoint Public Prosecutors.­(1) The 5* * 6[Provincial Government] may appoint,
generally, or in any case, or for any specified class of cases, in any local area, one or more officers to be
called Public Prosecutors.

 (2) 7* * 8[Officer­in­charge of prosecution in the district] may, in the absence of the Public Prosecutor,
or where no Public Prosecutor has been appointed, appoint any other person, not being an officer of police
below 9[such rank as the 6[Provincial Government] may prescribe in this behalf] to be Public Prosecutor for
the purpose of 10[any case].
