CHAPTER:

393. Not to be executed by instalments Exemptions.­ No sentence of whipping shall be
executed by instalments: and none of the following persons shall be punishable with whipping,
namely :__

 (a) females ;

 (b) males sentenced to death or to 1[imprisonment for life], 2* * * , or to imprisonment for
 more than five years;

 (c) males whom the Court considers to be more than forty­five years of age.
