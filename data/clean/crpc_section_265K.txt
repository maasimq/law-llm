ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 265K
TITLE: Power of Court to acquit accused at any stage. Nothing in this Chapter shall be
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

265K. Power of Court to acquit accused at any stage. Nothing in this Chapter shall be
deemed to prevent a Court from acquitting an accused at any stage of the case, if, after hearing the
prosecutor and the accused and for reasons to be recorded, it considers that there is no probability
of the accused being convicted of any offence.
