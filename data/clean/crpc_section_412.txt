ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 412
TITLE: No appeal in certain cases when accused pleads guilty.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

412. No appeal in certain cases when accused pleads guilty. Notwithstanding anything hereinbefore
contained where an accused person has pleaded guilty and has been convicted by 3[a High Court], a Court
of Session 4* * *or Magistrate of the first class on such plea, there shall be no appeal except as to the extent
or legality of the sentence.
