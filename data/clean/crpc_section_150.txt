ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 150
TITLE: Information of design to commit such offences.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

150. Information of design to commit such offences. Every police­officer receiving information of a
design to commit any cognizable offence shall communicate such information to the police­officer to whom
he is subordinate, and to any other officer whose duty it is to prevent or take cognizance of the commission
of any such offence.
