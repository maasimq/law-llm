ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION/ARTICLE: 99E
TITLE: Evidence to prove nature or tendency of newspapers.
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

99E. Evidence to prove nature or tendency of newspapers. On the hearing of any such application
with reference to any newspaper, any copy of such newspaper may be given in evidence in aid of the proof
of the nature or tendency of the words, signs or visible representations contained in such newspaper, 5[in
respect of which the order of forfeiture was made].
