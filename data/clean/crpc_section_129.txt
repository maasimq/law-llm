ACT: Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898
SECTION: 129
TITLE: Use of military force. If any such assembly cannot be otherwise dispersed, and if it is necessary
CHAPTER: 
============================================================

129. Use of military force. If any such assembly cannot be otherwise dispersed, and if it is necessary
for the public security that it should be dispersed, 8[the police officer of the highest rank not below an
Assistant Superintendent, or Deputy Superintendent of Police] who is present may cause it to be dispersed
by 9[the armed forces].
