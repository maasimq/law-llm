135. Person to whom order is addressed to obey or show cause or claim jury. The person against
whom such order is made shall__

(a) perform, within the time 1[and in the manner] specified in the order, the act directed thereby ; or

(b) appear in accordance with such order and either show cause against the same, or apply to the
Magistrate by whom it was made to appoint a jury to try whether the same is reasonable and
proper.
