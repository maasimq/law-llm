16. Power to frame rules for guidance of 5[Magistrates and] Benches. The 6[Provincial Government]
may 7[:], from time to time, make rules8 consistent with this Code for the guidance of Magistrates 5[all
Magistrates and] Benches in any district respecting the following subjects:__

 (a) the classes of cases to be tried;

 (b) the times and places of sitting;

 (c) the constitution of the Bench for conducting trials;

 (d) the mode of settling differences of opinion which may arise between the Magistrates in session
 [:]5

 (e) 5[The mode and manner of conduting raids and trial on the spot.]
